The glossary cards are generated from the Japanese MDN Web Docs glossary in the [mdn/translated-content repository](https://github.com/mdn/translated-content/tree/main/files/ja/glossary). The English-language glossary and related source material are maintained in the [mdn/content repository](https://github.com/mdn/content).

MDN Web Docs content is generally available under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike (CC BY-SA) license. Individual pages may contain exceptions or separately licensed material; consult the license and attribution information on the relevant MDN page. Each generated card includes a link to its MDN source page.

The app displays the first explanatory paragraph of each Japanese glossary page as a study-card definition. Markdown formatting and MDN macros are converted to plain text for display. The app is not an official MDN product.
